Why not just a booking calendar?

Trust

Identity verified, certifications checked against what each resource requires before a request even reaches the owner.

Fairness

Weekly caps, open-request limits and an explainable priority score stop one person or team monopolising scarce machines.

Accountability

Before/after condition reports, damage and delay incidents, disputes and a tamper-evident activity log.

Human control

Owners decide. The system only recommends, auto-approves where the owner allows it, and always explains why.
